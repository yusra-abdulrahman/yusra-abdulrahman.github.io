About Dr. Yusra Abdulrahman

Assistant Professor | Deputy Director, Aerospace Engineering, Khalifa University. Affiliated with the Advanced Research and Innovation Center (ARIC).

Her work with UAE aerospace companies dates to 2014 and focuses on automation, inspection and artificial intelligence.

Education

  • PhD in Interdisciplinary Engineering, Masdar Institute of Science and Technology in collaboration with Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT).
  • MSc, Masdar Institute in collaboration with MIT.
  • BSc with honors, University of Arizona, USA.

Research projects

Automated blade sorting

Robotic handling, load-cell weighing and AI-based vision for aero-engine blade sorting and mass balance.

Automated chord measurement

A robotic arm and laser profiler measure blade chords through a ROS-based interface, developed with Lufthansa Technik and SANAD Aerotech.

Aerospace engine blade inspection

Deep learning combines synthetic and real datasets with robotic handling and image acquisition for autonomous surface inspection.

For these three projects, the university lists Dr. Abdulrahman as Principal Investigator, with SANAD Aerotech and Mubadala Aerospace Company sponsorship, January–December 2024.

Infrared thermography for aerospace NDT

Integration of infrared thermography and emerging technologies for nondestructive testing. KU internal funding.

Plant AI and AerialYield

Research in collaboration with Silal, supported by KU external funding.
